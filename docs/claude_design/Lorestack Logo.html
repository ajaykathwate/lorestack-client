Construction

Built on a tight grid — three bars and a dot.

The idea

A story has layers. So does a publication. The mark stacks title, body, foundation — three bars that double as the strokes of an italic L.

Why it works
  • 01 · Reads at 16px and 256px equally well.
  • 02 · Works in gradient, mono, or outline.
  • 03 · The dot is the wordmark's period — visual rhyme.
  • 04 · No serif-tracing of the actual letter L — the bars say "stack" first.
